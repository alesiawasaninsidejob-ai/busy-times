    How this is estimated

    Sourced

    • Business identity: Dunn Brothers Coffee is Suite 100 (AT&T and Jimmy John's, Ste 300, share the street address). Hours: Mon–Fri 5:30–5, Sat–Sun 6:30–5.
    • Hourly popular-times profile for all 7 days, from a page mirroring Google's relative busyness scale (100 = the busiest it usually gets). Collected 2026-10-14. No second mirror with hourly values was found, and no live ("now") data or sub-hourly data is publicly available.
    • Typical visit is about 1 hour (Wanderlog). Reviews mention long waits at busy times, and a 2025 review says weekday mornings are staffed by 1–2 baristas.
    • 2026-27 New Richmond school times: MS/HS 8:00–3:10, with drop-off from 7:30. Elementary 8:25–3:25, with drop-off from 7:55. Breaks come from the district calendar.

    Interpolated / estimated

    • 10-minute values: each sourced hourly value is placed at the middle of its hour (e.g. 9:30) and joined with a monotone cubic curve (Fritsch–Carlson), so the curve never overshoots the data. Each slot takes the value at its center. These are estimates.
    • Opening and closing: slots before opening (e.g. before 5:30) and from 5:00 PM on are 0. The 5 AM value is held flat for 5:30–6:00.
    • School-day bumps of +3 or +4 points at 7:30–8:00, 8:00–8:45 and 3:10–3:40 on approximate school days (Sep 1, 2026–Jun 3, 2027 weekdays, minus breaks). The times are sourced; the size of each bump is an estimate. Caution: the popular-times data may predate the 2026-27 schedule change. Dates outside 2026-27 are treated as non-school days.
    • Monthly multipliers and holiday/event factors (see the main page).
    • Confidence: MEDIUM means interpolated from sourced hours. LOW means within 30 min of open/close, an estimated adjustment applies, or a holiday/event day. The app never shows HIGH for a 10-minute slot.
    • Formula: in-store = interpolated sourced curve × monthly estimate × holiday estimate + school bump, clamped to 1–100 while open.

    Delivery orders (separate metric)
